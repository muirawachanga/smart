About SmartO

SmartO is an ordering and fulfillment platform designed to connect the customer shopping journey with the operational work behind each order.

Our purpose

Make ordering clearer for customers and give business teams a shared view of orders, stock, payments, and delivery.

Our direction

Build toward more helpful, data-informed commerce while keeping important business actions secure and reviewable.

Our approach

Start with dependable ordering and fulfillment workflows, then add intelligent assistance when the required data and integrations are ready.

Our values

Security, reliability, usability, and responsible use of business data guide the platform's development.

Customer benefits

A clearer path to browse, place, and follow an order as the customer features are completed.

Development status

SmartO is being released in stages. AI recommendations, forecasting, route optimization, and outbound SMS are not currently available.
