Plan a delivery group

Use an unassigned delivery with verified coordinates as the starting order. The nearest idle rider with a location shared in the last 30 minutes is suggested. Nearby unassigned deliveries from the same area are grouped.

Distances are straight-line estimates, not road directions. They exclude pickup travel, traffic, obstacles and rider capacity; review the group before assigning it.
